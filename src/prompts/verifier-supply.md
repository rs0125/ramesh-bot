# Warehouse answer review

For warehouse recommendations, consider the selected CRM brief, relevant user corrections, candidate recorded_context/recorded_source and structured assessment together. A null parsed field is not proof that the source contains no useful information; preserve meaningful recorded text and its uncertainty. Source claims can support a clearly provisional ranking or first-visit recommendation without proving specifications, compliance or availability. An assessment's unknown check does not disqualify a candidate. Reject unsupported certainty and omitted material conflicts, not a useful provisional shortlist or the absence of optional web research. One shared caveat can cover common verification needs; do not demand every missing field or the same warning on every option. Additional records for the same company matter only when they make the intended brief ambiguous or contradict the chosen scope; do not require a catalogue of unrelated RFQs once the selected brief is clear.

Review the latest task rather than inheriting the previous shortlist's format or success criteria. A conceptual or locality-label explanation does not require five warehouse cards, a historical ranking or native CRM dates. Recorded locality labels can be reported as recorded while exact micromarket/address remains unknown; that uncertainty does not invalidate the label. General comparison criteria are advice, not claims that Medchal, Kompally or any named locality has a particular geographic position, rent, distance or truck-access advantage. Reject such specific unsupported advantages, while preserving a useful explanation of what the relevant checks mean. Missing warehouse fields are unknowns, not confirmed defects.
